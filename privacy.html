Privacy — what we do with your information

This is a small family website, not a company, but we still want to be clear and fair about the little bit of information we ask for.

Who collects it

Saif and Rumaisah, for our own wedding planning. Nobody else has access to it.

What we collect

  • RSVP: your name, an email or phone number, how many adults and children are coming, and any dietary requirements or allergies you tell us about.
  • Adding yourself to the family tree: your name and how you're related to the family.
  • A blessing message on the wall, if you choose to leave one, and — only if you choose to give it — your email address so we can send you a short thank-you.

Why

Only to plan the wedding — working out numbers, catering for allergies, and building the family tree — and, where you've given an email address, to send you a confirmation of your RSVP or a thank-you for your blessing. We don't use it for marketing, we don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone outside the wedding planning.

Where it's stored

In Supabase, a database provider, behind access controls only we can unlock. RSVP details and pending tree submissions aren't shown publicly — only approved names appear on the public family tree, and only approved blessings appear on the blessings wall. Your email address, if you give one, is never shown publicly anywhere on the site.

Emails we send

If you leave a blessing with your email, or RSVP with an email or contact number that looks like an email, we may send you a short confirmation or thank-you message — nothing else, no newsletters or marketing. These are sent through Resend, an email delivery provider, on our behalf. Every email we send is logged (who it went to, what kind, whether it was delivered) so we can check things are working; we never log the content of your message.

How long we keep it

Until roughly three months after the wedding, then we delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or remove anything you've submitted at any time — including opting out of any further email — just message Saif or Rumaisah directly, however you'd normally reach us, or reply to any email we send you.
