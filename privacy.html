Privacy — what we do with your information

This is a small family website, not a company, but we still want to be clear and fair about the little bit of information we ask for.

Who collects it

Saif and Rumaisah, for our own wedding planning. A few services handle the information on our behalf, only to run the site: Supabase (database hosting, in London), GitHub Pages (website hosting), Resend (email delivery, only once we switch it on) and Namecheap (for a shared mailbox, only once we set one up). The site's typefaces are hosted on the site itself, so visiting it does not send anything to Google. The venue receives headcounts and, where needed for catering, dietary requirements — nothing else. The website's code is public on GitHub, but it contains no guest data.

What we collect

  • RSVP: your name, an email or phone number, whether you can come, how many adults and children are coming, the children's ages if you give them (we do not ask for children's names), any dietary requirements or allergies you tell us about, and an optional note for us.
  • Adding someone to the family tree: their name, who they connect to, how they are related, which side of the family, whether they are a child, and, if you want to, your own name so we know who to thank.
  • A blessing on the wall, if you choose to leave one: your name, your message and, only if you choose to give it, your email address so we can send you a short thank-you.
  • Technical logs: our hosting providers keep standard logs of visits, which include IP addresses. We don't use these ourselves.
  • An activity log: we keep a private log of admin and system events (for example approving a tree entry or a blessing, or a new RSVP). It can include guest names and headcounts. Only we can see it.

Adding other people to the tree

Anyone using the site can add a name to the family tree, including relatives and children who haven't been asked. Every entry waits for our approval before it appears, and we check it before approving. If you've been added and want your entry (or your child's) removed or changed, tell us and we will do it. See “Your choices” for how to ask. Please only add people you know and who would be happy to be included.

Children

On the public family tree, children appear only as “Little one”. Family members who enter the shared family code can see children's real names. That code is stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value, never as plain text.

Dietary and allergy details

These can be health-related, so we keep them only if you tick the consent box when you RSVP. You can withdraw that consent at any time and we will delete them.

Services and transfers

Supabase hosts our database in London. GitHub Pages hosts the website. Resend would send our emails, and Namecheap would host a mailbox, but only once we switch them on. Some of these providers are based in the US. Where information goes outside the UK, we rely on the providers' standard contractual protections. Hosting providers also keep their own technical logs, including IP addresses.

Why

Only to plan the wedding — working out numbers, catering for allergies, and building the family tree — and, where you've given an email address, to contact you about the wedding. We don't use it for marketing, we don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone outside the wedding planning.

Where it's stored

In Supabase, a database provider, behind access controls only we can unlock. RSVP details and pending tree submissions aren't shown publicly — only approved names appear on the public family tree, and only approved blessings appear on the blessings wall. Suspicious blessings are held for approval before they appear. The email address on a blessing is stored but never shown publicly anywhere on the site.

Emails

We may email you about the wedding and nothing else — no newsletters, no marketing. If we send email, it goes through Resend, an email delivery provider, on our behalf. Emails we send are logged (who it went to, what kind, whether it was delivered) so we can check things are working; we never log the content of your message.

How long we keep it

Nothing is deleted automatically. About three months after the wedding we aim to delete all RSVPs, blessings, email logs and the activity log. (The wedding date isn't fixed yet, so that timing may move.) Family-tree entries are removed separately, on request. You can ask us to delete anything you've submitted at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or remove anything you've submitted at any time — including opting out of any email — just message Saif or Rumaisah directly, however you'd normally reach us. We will answer requests to see, correct or delete your information within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
